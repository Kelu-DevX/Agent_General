## Terms & privacy (plain language, full text)

**The short version:** be kind, post your own photos, keep it wholesome.

1. **Your photos.** Only upload photos you took. By uploading, you let 7B display them publicly on the wall. They're still yours.
2. **Moderation.** Every photo is reviewed by a human before it posts. We can decline or remove anything, anytime — usually for privacy, safety, or vibe. No hard feelings.
3. **People in photos.** Don't post other people without their OK, and never other people's kids.
4. **Age.** You need to be 13 or older to make an account.
5. **Privacy.** We collect your email and display name. That's it. The wall shows your display name only — never your email, never your location. Photos are scrubbed of location data before they're stored. We never sell your info.
6. **Prizes.** Seasonal prizes are small, fun, and mailed — winners are contacted by email. Not a lottery, just neighbors playing a game.
7. **Down the road.** If local businesses ever appear on a 7B page, "sponsored" means they paid for the spot — it's not a recommendation.
8. **The spirit of the thing.** 7B exists to make Sandpoint a little more fun and a little more connected. Play like your grandma's watching the wall. (She might be.)
